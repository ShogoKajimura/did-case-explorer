Policies

Terms of use, privacy, and prohibited uses.

0. Nature of the service

This service, the DID Case-Literature Companion Resource, is not an official university service. It is an unofficial service operated privately, for research purposes, by the operator. Neither the operator nor the operator's university bears any responsibility for any disadvantage or damage arising from use of this service. Its purpose is to support review research on DID/MPD case literature. It is not intended to publish or redistribute the full text of copyrighted works.

  • Data controller: Shogo Kajimura, an individual researcher. The university is not the operator of this service.
  • Legal basis for processing: the submitter's consent, given at submission, for the purpose of academic review research.
  • Retention: the uploaded PDF is deleted within 90 days of submission; derived material (OCR text, searchable PDF, structured Markdown, summary) within 24 months. Deletions are logged, and earlier deletion can be requested at any time.
  • Where processing happens: on the operator's machine in Japan.
  • Contact, deletion requests, rights-infringement claims: the form in section 5 below.
  • Ethical review: the service collects no new research data from human participants; it processes already-published literature supplied by its submitters. It has therefore not been submitted for institutional review board approval. The university's administrative and legal contacts have confirmed that operating it raises no objection on their side.

0b. Scope of what may be submitted, and de-identification

Only already-published academic literature may be submitted. Unpublished clinical records and patient files must not be sent. Because submissions are limited in this way, the service does not perform additional de-identification of the material it receives; material that would require de-identification does not belong here. This limit is stated in the consent items and is enforced by the server, which refuses a submission that does not affirm it.

1. What you receive

Your PDF is processed internally by OCR and analysis for review research. What is returned to you is a research-reference summary, delivered as a PDF, with bibliographic details. The full text, a searchable PDF, and the internal OCR output are not returned. The submitted full text and its OCR output are not published or redistributed; they are held only in a private, token-protected area. The returned summary is not guaranteed to be accurate and may contain errors; consult the original publication for any authoritative use.

3. Privacy policy

  • Information collected: the submitted PDF, the lawful-access confirmation, optionally your name, email address, affiliation, and bibliographic details, the IP address at submission, and an access token used to manage status.
  • Purposes: OCR processing and analysis for review research; notifying you of status and returning the summary; preventing misuse (for example, rate limiting).
  • Data minimisation: only the PDF and the lawful-access confirmation are required to start processing. Everything else is optional.
  • Consent: name, email, and affiliation are collected only when you enter them of your own accord.
  • Security: collected information and outputs are protected in a local environment using disk encryption, owner-only access permissions, access control by tokens and an API key, and encrypted backups. They are not held in any public location.
  • Third parties: provision of your personal data to third parties is not intended.
  • No external AI processing: document content is never sent to an external cloud AI service. OCR, summarisation, and relevance screening all run locally on the operator's machine. The only thing sent outside is a DOI or title string, used to look up bibliographic details.
  • Retention and deletion: uploaded PDFs are deleted within 90 days of submission and derived material within 24 months; deletions are recorded in an audit log. Earlier deletion can be requested at any time through the form on the right.

4. Prohibited uses

  • Submitting anything other than already-published academic literature, in particular unpublished clinical records or patient files.
  • Submitting improperly obtained content (copyrighted works obtained without a lawful basis).
  • Submitting or using content in a way that infringes rights without the rights holder's permission.
  • Using the service for purposes other than research.
  • Using the service in a way that violates law or public order.
  • Attempting unauthorised access, imposing excessive load, or otherwise interfering with the service.
