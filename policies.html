Terms, privacy & takedown

How this service handles submissions, data, and rights requests.

This is an unofficial research service operated by an individual, not an official university service. It returns a research-reference summary of each submission and does not redistribute full texts.

0. Nature of the service

This service, the DID Case-Literature Companion Resource, is not an official university service. It is an unofficial service operated privately, for research purposes, by the operator. Neither the operator nor the operator's university bears any responsibility for any damage arising from use of this service. Its purpose is to support review research on DID/MPD case literature. It is not intended to publish or redistribute the full text of copyrighted works.

1. What you receive

Your PDF is processed internally by OCR and analysis for review research. What is returned to you is a research-reference summary with bibliographic details. The full text, a searchable PDF, and the internal OCR output are not returned. The submitted full text and its OCR output are not published or redistributed; they are held only in a private, token-protected area. The returned summary is not guaranteed to be accurate and may contain errors; consult the original publication for any authoritative use.

2. Terms of use

  • The service is provided for research use only.
  • It is an unofficial service operated privately, not an official university service.
  • You represent that you have lawful access to the submitted PDF and the rights, or a lawful basis, necessary to provide it for research purposes.
  • The service imposes no additional copyright-based restriction on the returned summary, but does not guarantee its accuracy.
  • Neither the operator nor the operator's university bears any responsibility for damage caused to you or a third party by use of the service, nor gives any warranty as to the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose of the summary or OCR output.
  • If you use this service from outside Japan, you are responsible for complying with the copyright law and other applicable laws of your country.

3. Privacy policy

  • Information collected: the submitted PDF, the lawful-access confirmation, optionally your name, email address, affiliation, and bibliographic details, the IP address at submission, and an access token used to manage status.
  • Purposes: OCR processing and analysis for review research; notifying you of status and returning the summary; preventing misuse (for example, rate limiting).
  • Data minimisation: only the PDF and the lawful-access confirmation are required to start processing. Everything else is optional.
  • Consent: name, email, and affiliation are collected only when you enter them of your own accord.
  • Security: collected information and outputs are protected in a local environment using disk encryption, owner-only access permissions, access control by tokens and an API key, and encrypted backups. They are not held in any public location.
  • Third parties: provision of your personal data to third parties is not intended.
  • Deletion: deletion requests are handled through the contact below.

4. Prohibited uses

  • Submitting improperly obtained content (copyrighted works obtained without a lawful basis).
  • Submitting or using content in a way that infringes rights without the rights holder's permission.
  • Using the service for purposes other than research.
  • Using the service in a way that violates law or public order.
  • Attempting unauthorised access, imposing excessive load, or otherwise interfering with the service.

5. Takedown & rights-infringement contact

Rights holders may request removal of material, and anyone may report a rights infringement, using the form below. Please include enough detail to identify the item. We will act after review. Requests are also recorded so they are not lost.
