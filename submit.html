Restricted intake workflow

Submit a paper for OCR, bibliographic triage, and later review.

This page is the public-facing intake surface for scanned or hard-to-access DID/MPD case literature. Submitted PDFs are processed on a restricted backend and are not made public through this site by default.

PDF only Private OCR workflow Opt-in contributor credit
Read this before you submit Four things to know.
  1. Published literature only. Do not send unpublished clinical records or patient files.
  2. You receive a summary PDF, not the full text and not a searchable PDF of your original.
  3. Your file is deleted. The upload within 90 days, material derived from it within 24 months.
  4. This is an unofficial service run by an individual, not by the university. Neither accepts responsibility for the lawfulness of a submission, the accuracy of the summary, or any disadvantage arising from use.
